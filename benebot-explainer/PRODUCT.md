# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hackathon judges, healthcare builders, AI engineers, collaborators, and curious people who want to understand how BeneBot turns one synthetic medical bill into a trustworthy bilingual conversation and a standards-based follow-up workflow.

## Product Purpose

Make BeneBot's full architecture learnable and shareable. The explainer should reveal the exact evidence chain, clarify which decisions are deterministic versus generative, demonstrate the historical-versus-current time boundary, and give visitors the artifacts needed to inspect the design for themselves.

## Brand Personality

Warm, mechanical, vigilant. BeneBot should feel unusually joyful for healthcare software while remaining exact about evidence, uncertainty, privacy, and server-confirmed actions.

## Anti-references

Avoid sterile enterprise healthcare portals, generic teal wellness branding, soft-focus AI gradients, repetitive feature-card grids, unexplained architecture diagrams, and claims that collapse live eligibility, historical adjudication, or generated language into one source of truth.

## Design Principles

1. Show the chain, not just the outcome.
2. Make time and provenance impossible to miss.
3. Let deterministic controls look structurally different from generative narration.
4. Treat English and Spanish as equal first-class paths.
5. Make every claim inspectable through a downloadable artifact or official source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic HTML, full keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable line lengths, touch-friendly controls, and explanations that do not rely on color alone. Preserve the text path everywhere voice is discussed.
