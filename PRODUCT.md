# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BeneBot serves patients trying to understand one medical bill, especially older adults and people who prefer Spanish, plus provider billing teams receiving the follow-up. The patient is often anxious, may be using a phone or shared family computer, and needs a grounded explanation without surrendering more identifying information.

## Product Purpose

BeneBot explains how one historical claim produced a bill, optionally checks current benefits as a separate timestamped source, and turns unresolved questions into a confirmed staff workflow. The P0 succeeds when Jane can move from the synthetic bill into an English or Spanish web voice session and the staff view can prove the resulting work.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, and plainspoken. BeneBot treats the patient as capable and in control. The experience should feel energetic enough to counter billing anxiety while remaining precise about sources, dates, uncertainty, and external actions.

## Anti-references

Avoid sterile hospital portals, generic blue-and-white healthcare SaaS, victim imagery, infantilizing senior-care visuals, chatbot bubbles floating without context, and futuristic voice interfaces that hide the bill or the text fallback.

## Design Principles

1. Put the patient in the hero role.
2. Make voice the clearest next step, with text always available.
3. Use visual separation to reinforce historical claim, current benefits, and current balance as different sources.
4. Show confidence through direct language and strong composition, never through unsupported certainty.
5. Carry one recognizable visual vocabulary from the public pitch through the bill, conversation, and staff proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, high contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, English and Spanish labels, and text alternatives for voice. Do not rely on color alone for source or status meaning.
